# Intrinsic–Extrinsic Coupling in Learning Dynamics

**Research and Reproducibility Repository**

**Author:** Qinyou Wang  
**Paper:** [Intrinsic-Extrinsic Coupling in Learning Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30185)  
**arXiv:** 2609.30185 (2026)

## Overview

A learner's current behavior does not necessarily determine how it will respond to future training.

This research investigates the coupling between intrinsic learning-state structure and extrinsic learning interventions, with particular attention to hidden differences between learners that agree on current observations.

The paper develops an operational framework connecting observation-relative learning-state fibers, constrained interventions, continuation-conditioned value, matched interaction identification, and closed-loop coordination.

## Research Contributions

The paper studies three distinct levels of learning intervention: local admissibility, continuation-conditioned intervention value, and complete-policy performance.

It introduces a constrained classifier-head intervention designed to preserve current logits while repairing specified historical margins.

Matched four-cell experiments identify readout-specific interactions between intrinsic interventions and alternative external training continuations.

The empirical analysis includes class-incremental learning, experience replay, output distillation, RoBERTa, and SGDW optimizer dynamics. The results distinguish identified coupling from uniformly positive synergy or guaranteed improvements in complete-policy performance.
